The problem

How Althais solves it

Althais listens to the visit, spoken or typed, and turns it into a structured SOAP note you edit and sign.

Codes with the reasons behind them

Smart Coding

The problem

Coding from a free-text note means guessing at levels and modifiers, and undercoding quietly costs the practice.

How Althais solves it

Althais reads the note and suggests ICD-10 and CPT codes, each with a confidence score and the documentation behind it.

  • A confidence score on every code
  • Accept, reject or add your own
  • Modifier and specificity prompts

Clean Claims

The problem

Bundling conflicts and missing fields are found after a denial, weeks later, when the visit is long forgotten.

How Althais solves it

Every claim is checked against CMS NCCI edits while the note is still open, then tracked from submission to payment.

  • NCCI edits checked on every claim
  • Sent only after your team approves it
  • Denials flagged, with an appeal letter drafted
